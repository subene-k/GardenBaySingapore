### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: THE TRANSFORMATION VISION

The **Gardens by the Bay Interactive Digital Visitor Experience** is an intelligent, multi-touchpoint visitor application designed to modernize the visitor journey across Singapore’s premier 101-hectare horticultural attraction. 

It seamlessly bridges **pre-visit web planning**, **on-ground interactive touch kiosks**, and **in-park handheld mobile exploration** without requiring any app store downloads or visitor account logins.

### SLIDE 3 / SECTION 3: DEEP DIVE — ALL CORE FEATURES

#### FEATURE 1: IMMERSIVE EXPLORE & PARK ARRIVAL HUB
* **Live Park Vitals Bar**: Displays operating hours (**5:00 AM – 2:00 AM daily**), outdoor free admission policy, factual Supertree counts (**18 total: 12 in Supertree Grove, 6 across Golden & Silver Gardens**), and climate-controlled indoor biomes.
* **Curated Attraction Spotlight Cards**: High-impact cards for Flower Dome, Cloud Forest, Supertree Grove, and Floral Fantasy with pricing, indoor/outdoor tags, and direct map pinpointing.
* **Garden Rhapsody Advisory**: Live schedule for the signature evening light and sound show (**7:45 PM & 8:45 PM daily**).
* **Quick-Jump Launchers**: One-click actions into Interactive Map, Curated Itineraries, Kiosk Mode, and Visitor Amenities.

#### FEATURE 2: INTERACTIVE ILLUSTRATED MAP ENGINE
* **Illustrated Park Cartography**: Preserves the official, high-resolution illustrated artistic map of Gardens by the Bay with accurate geographical landmark placement.
* **Fluid Viewport Navigation**: Smooth panning, stepped zoom (1.0x to 3.5x), pinch-to-zoom for mobile screens, double-tap zoom, reset view, and fullscreen immersion.
* **6 Dedicated Category Filters**:
  1. *All Highlights*: Full-park panoramic view of all 14 attractions.
  2. *Cooled Conservatories*: Flower Dome, Cloud Forest, Floral Fantasy.
  3. *Supertrees & Aerial*: Supertree Grove, OCBC Skyway, Supertree Observatory.
  4. *Outdoor Themed Gardens*: Heritage Gardens, Serene Garden, Sun Pavilion, The Meadow, The Canyon.
  5. *Lakes & Nature*: Dragonfly & Kingfisher Lakes, Kingfisher Wetlands, Bay East Garden.
  6. *Family & Children*: Far East Organization Children's Garden (Water Play & Treehouses).
* **Smart Clustered Pin Placement**: Automated spatial offsetting prevents overlapping pins in dense zones like Supertree Grove and keeps tooltips visible at screen edges.
* **Real-Time Live Search**: Instant filter by attraction name, keyword, or plant collection.
* **Transit & Amenities Layer**: Pinpoints Bayfront MRT (Exit B underground linkway), Gardens by the Bay MRT (TE22), Main Visitor Centre, EV shuttle boarding points, nursing rooms, lockers, and restrooms.
* **Visual Itinerary Route**: Dynamic glowing SVG polyline with animated dashed paths connecting selected stops in order.

#### FEATURE 3: ATTRACTION INFORMATION DOSSIER & BOTANICAL AUDIO COMPANION
* **Verified Attraction Intel**: Verified operating hours, admission pricing (Standard rates vs. Free outdoor access), recommended visit duration, and biome climate data (e.g., 23°C – 25°C indoor cooled biomes).
* **Multilingual Botanical Audio Companion**:
  * Available across **English, 中文, Bahasa Melayu, and தமிழ்**.
  * Generates a calming, harmonic pentatonic chime soundscape using the browser's Web Audio synthesizer.
  * Natural spoken voice narration with automatic voice selection based on the visitor’s language.
* **Curated Highlights & Insider Photography Tips**: Exact photo angles, Cloud Forest misting hours, and sunset timing recommendations.
* **Arrival Guidance**: Specific transit exits and nearest walking paths.
* **Action Buttons**: "Pin on Map", "Add to My Visit", and official website verification link.

#### FEATURE 4: SMART ITINERARY BUILDER & CURATED TOURS
* **5 Pre-Built Signature Itineraries**:
  1. **1-Hour Express Tour**: Cloud Forest & Supertree Grove (60 mins, 2 stops).
  2. **2-Hour Signature Experience**: Flower Dome, Cloud Forest & OCBC Skyway (120 mins, 3 stops).
  3. **3-Hour Complete Immersion**: Conservatories, Skyway, Supertree Observatory & Heritage Gardens (180 mins, 5 stops).
  4. **Family & Kids Adventure**: Children’s Garden Water Play, Cloud Forest & Floral Fantasy (150 mins, 3 stops).
  5. **Golden Hour & Night Photography**: Supertree Observatory, OCBC Skyway, Dragonfly Lake & Garden Rhapsody (110 mins, 4 stops).
* **Custom Dynamic Builder**: Add or remove any attraction, reorder stops up/down, and inspect real-time cumulative duration estimates.
* **Map Route Synchronization**: Displays the planned route directly on the illustrated map.

#### FEATURE 5: ON-GROUND TOUCH KIOSK CONCIERGE
* **Purpose-Built Touchscreen UI**: High-contrast, large-button design optimized for public touchscreen terminals (e.g., Terminal #04 at Visitor Arrival Plaza).
* **1-Touch Destination Locator**: Immediate one-tap pinpointing to primary destinations.
* **Language Switcher**: Dedicated prominent buttons for Singapore's 4 national languages.
* **Automated Inactivity Timeout**: Gentle modal alert after 60 seconds of inactivity, resetting to the default welcome state at 75 seconds for the next park guest.
* **Mobile Handoff QR**: High-resolution QR code allowing visitors to scan and instantly carry the mobile visitor guide on their phone.

#### FEATURE 6: UNIVERSAL QR CROSS-DEVICE CONTINUATION
* **Seamless Mobile Loading**: Scanning from either the on-ground kiosk or website header/footer **always loads the responsive mobile website directly** on the visitor's phone (never the kiosk interface).
* **State Preservation**: Automatically transfers active itinerary stops, language preferences, and viewed attractions through lightweight URL parameters.
* **Instant Welcome Feedback**: Scanned visitors receive an immediate confirmation banner: *"Connected from Park Kiosk: Your mobile visitor guide & interactive map are ready."*

#### FEATURE 7: 4-LANGUAGE MULTILINGUAL INCLUSIVITY
* **English (EN)**: Complete UI, attraction details, and spoken voice companion.
* **Chinese (中文 / ZH)**: Complete UI, attraction details, and spoken voice companion.
* **Malay (Bahasa Melayu / MS)**: Complete UI, attraction details, and spoken voice companion.
* **Tamil (தமிழ் / TA)**: Complete UI, attraction details, and spoken voice companion.
